About Shivaputra Travels

Your trusted travel partner for sacred pilgrimages, family holidays, and executive mobility across India.

Our Ethos

Travel Grounded in Devotion, Safety & Reliability

Named with reverence to Lord Shiva, Shivaputra Travels was founded with a clear vision: to ensure that pilgrimage journeys across South India are conducted with the utmost peace, punctuality, and comfort.

From same-day Tirupati Balaji VIP darshans to the holy circuits of Kumbakonam, Rameswaram, Madurai, and Srisailam, we eliminate the stress of logistics so devotees can focus entirely on their prayers.

Our fleet also powers family holiday escapes across Ooty, Kodaikanal, Munnar, and Pondicherry, as well as dependable corporate ground transit for major enterprises.
